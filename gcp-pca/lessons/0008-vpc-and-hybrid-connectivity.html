GCP PCA — Lesson 8 of 17

VPC, Shared VPC, and hybrid connectivity

A VPC is your private network in GCP. Shared VPC lets multiple teams use one network safely. Hybrid connectivity links that network back to your on-prem data center.

Simple example: a VPC is like a private office building. Shared VPC is one building with several company floors — everyone uses the same front door and hallways, but each floor keeps its own keys and budget. Cloud VPN is a locked tunnel through the public street. Cloud Interconnect is a private road straight into the building — no public street at all.

VPC

A VPC is a global resource. Its subnets are regional, not zonal — one subnet covers every zone in that region. Every project needs one, default or custom.

Shared VPC (short)

One "host" project owns the network. Other "service" projects attach to it and use its subnets, while each service project keeps its own IAM and billing. Good fit: one network team, many app teams.

Hybrid connectivity: Cloud VPN vs Cloud Interconnect

Both connect GCP to your on-prem network. Cloud VPN goes over the public internet, encrypted. Cloud Interconnect is a private physical link straight to Google's network.

Cloud VPN (HA)Cloud Interconnect
PathPublic internet, encryptedPrivate link, bypasses internet
Bandwidth~1–3 Gbps per tunnel10–400 Gbps circuits
Setup timeFast — minutes to hoursSlow — needs a physical circuit
Wins whenFast setup, moderate bandwidthSustained high throughput, lowest latency
Exam trap: Dedicated Interconnect needs your own equipment inside a Google colocation facility. If the scenario says "no data center presence near a Google site," Dedicated is ruled out — the answer is Partner Interconnect (goes through a provider, no colo needed), not "add more Dedicated circuits."

Quiz — Lesson 8

Q1. In GCP, is a VPC subnet zonal or regional?

B. Unlike AWS, GCP subnets are regional — they span every zone in that region.

Q2. A company needs a link live in 2 weeks, moderate bandwidth, no colocation presence. Best fit?

B. Cloud VPN is the fastest to stand up and needs no colocation presence — the right fit for moderate bandwidth on a tight deadline.

Q3. Why would a company choose Shared VPC over giving each team its own separate VPC?

A. That's exactly Shared VPC's purpose — one network, centrally managed, with each service project keeping its own IAM and billing.

Q4. A company has rack space in a Google colocation facility and needs sustained 40 Gbps to on-prem. Best fit?

A. They already have colo presence, and Dedicated Interconnect circuits (10–100 Gbps, bundled higher) comfortably cover 40 Gbps with the lowest latency. Cloud VPN tops out around 1–3 Gbps per tunnel — far short.

Source

cloud.google.com/network-connectivity/docs/how-to/choose-product — official connectivity decision guide.

Recap
  • VPC = global resource, subnets are regional.
  • Shared VPC = one network, many teams, separate IAM/billing per team.
  • Cloud VPN = fast setup, public internet, moderate bandwidth.
  • Cloud Interconnect = private link, high bandwidth, needs colo presence (or a partner if you don't have one).
