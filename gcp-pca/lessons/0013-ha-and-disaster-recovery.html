HA and disaster recovery

HA and DR solve two different problems. HA keeps you running through a small failure. DR gets you back after a big one — with some data loss and downtime allowed.

Simple example: HA is a spare tire already mounted on the car. DR is the tow truck and repair shop you call when the whole car is gone.

HA basics

  • Multi-zone — survives one zone failing. Example: Cloud SQL HA (synchronous replica in another zone, ~60s failover), regional Persistent Disk, GKE regional clusters.
  • Multi-region — survives a whole region failing. Example: Spanner multi-region config, multi-region Cloud Storage buckets, global load balancing.

Multi-zone protects against a zone outage. Multi-region protects against a region outage. Pick based on what the scenario says you must survive.

The 4 DR patterns

Same 4 patterns as AWS. Cost and speed move together: faster recovery costs more.

PatternApprox. RPOApprox. RTOCost
Backup & restoreHoursHours$
Pilot lightMinutes–hoursTens of minutes$$
Warm standbyMinutesMinutes$$$
Multi-site active-activeNear-zeroNear-zero$$$$

RPO = how much data you can afford to lose. RTO = how long you can afford to be down. Read those two numbers in the scenario first — they tell you which row to pick.

Exam trap: a multi-region Cloud Storage bucket is not a DR plan by itself. It only protects your files. Your compute and your database still need their own backup or standby plan.

Try it — before looking below

Q1. A workload can lose at most 5 minutes of data and must be back within 10 minutes after a regional outage. Which pattern?

Warm standby. Too tight for pilot light (needs "tens of minutes" to scale up), but doesn't need the full cost of multi-site active-active.

Quiz — Lesson 13

Q1. Which pattern has the lowest RPO and RTO but the highest cost?

D. Multi-site active-active runs infrastructure continuously in multiple regions, so recovery is near-instant — but you pay for that capacity all the time.

Q2. True or false: a dual-region Cloud Storage bucket alone counts as a full DR plan.

A. Storage replication protects objects only. Compute Engine, GKE, and Cloud SQL need their own DR mechanism.

Q3. What does Cloud SQL HA protect against, and what does it NOT protect against?

A. Cloud SQL HA replicates synchronously to a standby in another zone — that's HA, not DR. A full region outage needs a cross-region replica or backup plan.

Recap
  • HA = survive a small failure. DR = recover from a big one, with a plan.
  • 4 DR patterns, in order of speed and cost: backup & restore → pilot light → warm standby → multi-site active-active.
  • Read the scenario's RPO/RTO numbers first — they point to the pattern.
  • Exam trap: replicated storage alone is not a full DR plan.
