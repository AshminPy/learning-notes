Exam structure

The Professional Cloud Architect exam has a fixed shape. Learn the shape first — it tells you where to spend your study time.

Simple example: think of the exam like a test with a hidden marks breakdown. Six sections, each worth a different amount. Study the heaviest sections first, same as revising for a test once you know the marks split.

Format

2 hours. 50–60 questions (multiple choice + multiple select). $200. Delivered online-proctored or at a testing center.

The 6 sections and their weight

#SectionWeight
1Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture25%
2Managing and provisioning infrastructure17.5%
3Designing for security and compliance17.5%
4Analyzing and optimizing technical & business processes15%
5Managing implementation12.5%
6Ensuring solution and operations excellence12.5%

Section 1 alone is a quarter of the exam. If time runs short, protect design/plan and security study time over cost or migration trivia.

Case studies

The official exam guide names 4 fictitious companies — Altostrat Media, Cymbal Retail, EHR Healthcare, KnightMotives Automotive — and states that some questions may refer you to a case study for extra context. Google does not publicly guarantee how many case studies or questions will appear, or which ones. Skim all four before exam day — 15 minutes now beats reading one cold under time pressure, regardless of the actual count.

Try it — before looking below

Q1. Which single section carries the most weight?

Section 1, Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture — 25%.

Q2. Does the official exam guide state exactly how many case studies or questions will reference them?

No — it only says "some of the questions may refer you to a case study." Treat any specific count or percentage you've seen elsewhere as an unverified estimate, not an official fact. Prepare by knowing all 4, not by betting on a number.

Source

Official PCA exam guide (PDF) — 7 pages, the source for every number above.

Recap
  • 2 hours, 50–60 questions, $200.
  • 6 sections, weighted 25/17.5/17.5/15/12.5/12.5 — design and security carry the most.
  • 4 case study companies; the official guide says only that "some questions may refer" to one — no confirmed count or percentage.
Stuck on a number? Re-read the exam guide link above — that's the habit the real exam requires anyway.
