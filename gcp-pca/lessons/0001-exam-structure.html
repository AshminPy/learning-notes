Exam structure

The Professional Cloud Architect exam has a fixed shape. Learn the shape first — it tells you where to spend your study time.

Simple example: think of the exam like a test with a hidden marks breakdown. Six sections, each worth a different amount. Study the heaviest sections first, same as revising for a test once you know the marks split.

Format

2 hours. 50–60 questions (multiple choice + multiple select). $200. Delivered online-proctored or at a testing center.

The 6 sections and their weight

#SectionWeight
1Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture25%
2Managing and provisioning infrastructure17.5%
3Designing for security and compliance17.5%
4Analyzing and optimizing technical & business processes15%
5Managing implementation12.5%
6Ensuring solution and operations excellence12.5%

Section 1 alone is a quarter of the exam. If time runs short, protect design/plan and security study time over cost or migration trivia.

Case studies

This applies to the standard PCA exam (the one most candidates take). The official certification page confirms: each standard exam includes 2 case studies, and case-study questions make up 20–30% of the exam. The exam guide names 4 available case studies — Altostrat Media, Cymbal Retail, EHR Healthcare, KnightMotives Automotive — but you don't know in advance which 2 of the 4 will appear. Know all four.

Renewal exam is different: if you're recertifying rather than taking the standard exam, the renewal exam includes just 1 case study (generative-AI-focused), making up 90–100% of that exam — almost the entire thing. Don't prep for renewal the same way you'd prep for the standard exam.

Try it — before looking below

Q1. Which single section carries the most weight?

Section 1, Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture — 25%.

Q2. On the standard exam, how many of the 4 available case studies actually appear, and what share of questions do they make up?

2 of the 4, making up 20–30% of the exam — but you don't know which 2 in advance, so know all four. (This is the standard exam; the renewal exam uses just 1 case study for 90–100% of its questions.)

Source

Official PCA exam guide (PDF) — domains, weights, and the 4 available case studies. Official certification page — exam format and case-study counts/percentages for both the standard and renewal exams.

Recap
  • 2 hours, 50–60 questions, $200.
  • 6 sections, weighted 25/17.5/17.5/15/12.5/12.5 — design and security carry the most.
  • Standard exam: 2 of 4 available case studies appear, 20–30% of questions. Renewal exam: 1 case study, 90–100% of questions.
Stuck on a number? Re-read the exam guide link above — that's the habit the real exam requires anyway.
