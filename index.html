Learning Notes

Short, simple lessons — one idea, one example, per page.

A free rapid-revision and active-recall companion for CKA and Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect candidates. It helps identify knowledge gaps but does not replace hands-on labs, official documentation, case-study practice, or full exam preparation. The CKA is performance-based — practise commands and troubleshooting in a real Kubernetes environment. The PCA exam requires architecture judgement and scenario-based decision-making — memorizing service definitions alone is not sufficient.

This is an independent study resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, Google Cloud, CNCF, or the Linux Foundation. Exam objectives, products, and services may change. Always verify details using the official exam guides and documentation.
